Mail Collection & Delivery Tracking

Our Service Process

From seamless onboarding to ongoing property visits, our four-stage operational cycle delivers consistent reassurance.

1

Initial Walkthrough

We conduct a comprehensive baseline onboarding tour with you to document shutoff valves, breaker locations, and property nuances.

2

Scheduled Inspections

Your assigned home watch inspector performs rigorous physical checks on your chosen frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).

3

Instant Digital Dispatch

Photographs and inspection checklists are synced to your private dashboard within 60 minutes of visit completion.

4

Direct Escalation

If an anomaly is spotted, you receive immediate telephone contact alongside photos, followed by coordinated vendor escort if requested.
