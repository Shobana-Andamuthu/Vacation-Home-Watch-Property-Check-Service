Observation Methodology

The Home-Watch Approach

Home watch is distinctly different from home security monitoring or maintenance contracting. It is a systematic, visual assessment of an unoccupied home to identify visible problems before they become costly catastrophes.

  • ✓ Sensory Assessment: Identifying unusual odors (dampness, gas, mold) that cameras and sensors cannot detect.
  • ✓ Mechanical Observation: Checking that refrigerators, humidifiers, and HVAC condensers cycle normally.
  • ✓ Water Line Maintenance: Exercising faucets and flushing toilets to replenish water seals and prevent dried gaskets.
