Proactive Damage Prevention

Why Home Watch Matters

Unoccupied luxury residences face silent vulnerabilities. Periodic physical walkthroughs prevent minor anomalies from cascading into catastrophic damage.

Primary Risk

Plumbing Ruptures & Trap Dryout

High-pressure supply lines and appliance hoses fail without warning. When unoccupied for weeks, dried plumbing traps also allow sewer gases and pests to enter living areas.

0.00 GPM
Static dial check confirms zero continuous municipal micro-flow
  • 15-min municipal water meter flow audit
  • P-trap freshwater circulation flush
  • Water heater pan & valve inspection
Climate Hazard

HVAC Failure & Toxic Mold

A tripped circuit breaker or clogged condensate line turns a luxury home into an incubator. High ambient humidity triggers mold growth across drywall, art, and cabinetry within 48 hours.

48 Hours
Average time for toxic mold to colonize following unmonitored AC failure
  • Dual digital temperature & humidity logging
  • Condensate drain line & pan float test
  • Thermostat baseline calibration check
Security & Weather

Perimeter Latches & Storm Impact

Strong coastal winds and thermal expansion loosen sliding doors and window seals. Strict homeowner insurance policies require verified physical inspections every 7 to 14 days to preserve coverage.

7–14 Days
Strict insurer vacancy clause requiring documented physical inspection
  • Physical 35-lb deadbolt torque confirmation
  • Roofline, gutter & downspout visual scan
  • Timestamped GPS photo dossier generation
